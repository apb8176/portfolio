About me

Early-career climate and disaster-risk researcher with a physics foundation and an Erasmus Mundus MSc in Coastal Hazards, Risks and Climate Change Adaptation, and two years of applied research experience across India and Europe.

I combine a strong GIS and remote-sensing foundation with quantitative risk modelling in Python (CLIMADA, uncertainty analysis) and coastal vulnerability index construction. My work focuses on coastal hazard analysis, disaster risk reduction, exposure assessment, and climate adaptation through geospatial evidence and reproducible scientific workflows.

Research works

    Python · CLIMADA · HPC · ENSO

The analysis integrated income-based displacement functions with high-resolution population exposure across 500+ administrative units and 33 countries.
